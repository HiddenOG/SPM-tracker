Privacy

SPMprocure360 · Special Piping Materials (Nig.) Ltd.

SPMprocure360 is the internal order-tracking system of Special Piping Materials (Nig.) Ltd. It is used only by our own staff, who sign in with accounts we issue.

What it reads

SPM Ask, the question-answering part of the system, reads the company's own mailbox, specialpiping@gmail.com, with read-only access, to answer staff questions about orders, clients and correspondence. It cannot send, change or delete any email.

What it keeps

  • Questions staff ask, and the answers given, are saved so the team can reopen them.
  • An answer may quote short parts of the emails it was drawn from, and lists those emails as its sources.
  • Nothing read from the mailbox is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who processes it

Answers are written with Anthropic's Claude, which receives only the question and the email extracts needed to answer it. Data is stored in our database hosted by Supabase, and the system runs on Railway.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
